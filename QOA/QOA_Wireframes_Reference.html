Operational Insights
🔥 Friday & Saturday 12–1pm consistently hits queue limit
For the last 4 weeks, orders exceeded your 10-order threshold during this window. Consider adding help or enabling auto-throttle at 11:45am on Fridays and Saturdays.
📋 Burrito Supreme takes 3× longer than any other item
Based on fulfillment times, Burrito Supreme adds ~8 min to those orders. During peak hours consider marking it unavailable or creating a rush-hour limited menu.
⭐ Top 3 items account for 71% of all orders
Carne Asada Taco, Horchata, and Fish Taco drive most revenue. Keeping these stocked during rush hours has the highest impact.
